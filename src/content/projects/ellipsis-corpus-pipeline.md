## What I built

- Automated extraction, annotation, and preprocessing pipelines that build structured multilingual corpora following interlinear glossing standards.
- A cross-linguistic analysis of ellipsis in Turkish, Korean, Mongolian, and Kazakh using large-scale corpus data, comparing syntactic patterns across the four languages.
- I presented the findings to UCSC faculty researchers; the resulting paper was accepted to a peer-reviewed linguistics journal.
